How OPN Vesting Works
A trustless, on-chain solution for token distribution
1

Create Schedule

Lock your ERC-20 tokens into the contract. Set the beneficiary, cliff period, and vesting duration. Tokens are transferred immediately.

2

Linear Unlock

After the cliff, tokens unlock linearly every second. At any time the beneficiary can see exactly how many tokens are available to claim.

3

Claim Anytime

Beneficiaries call release() to receive their vested tokens. If revocable, creators can cancel and reclaim unvested tokens.
